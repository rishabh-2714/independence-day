India

India, or Bharat, is a democratic nation in South Asia. It was formed from union and then partition under British rule, and follows a parliamentary bicameral system, with the currency of INR (₹/Rs/Rupee). It is currently ruled by the Bharatiya Janta Party (BJP), and was founded under the BJP's rival, the Indian National Congress (INC).
Indian Independence Day is celebrated on 15th August, and the original day was in the year 1947 CE.
